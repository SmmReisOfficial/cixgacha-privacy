## 1. Introduction

Welcome to CixGacha!

CixGacha is an anime-themed Discord gaming bot that offers interactive character name-chain games, competitive duels, collectible anime cards, trading, virtual currency, daily rewards, XP progression, and leaderboards.

We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to handling their information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CixGacha collects and processes, how that information is used, how long it is stored, and how users can request access to or deletion of their personal gameplay data.

By using CixGacha, you acknowledge the data processing practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

CixGacha processes certain information to provide its gaming features.

This information may include:

- **Discord User IDs:** Used to identify players and associate gameplay records with their Discord accounts.
- **Discord Server IDs:** Used to maintain separate game progress and information for different Discord communities.
- **Player Progress:** Experience points (XP), levels, scores, virtual currency, and leaderboard information.
- **Card Collections:** Anime character cards owned, earned, crafted, or traded by players.
- **Daily Rewards:** Reward claims, timestamps, and participation streaks.
- **Gameplay Messages:** Anime character names submitted during games and competitive duels.
- **Security Information:** Temporary message timing information, recent gameplay submissions, and anti-macro verification states.
- **Discord Profile Information:** Display names, avatars, and relevant server membership information when necessary for rankings, rewards, and gameplay features.

CixGacha does not require users to provide passwords, payment card information, or sensitive Discord account credentials.

## 3. Message Content Processing

CixGacha uses Discord message content to operate its interactive gaming features.

When users participate in the designated game channel or active duel threads, the bot processes submitted character names to:

- Validate character names against its database.
- Verify the required starting letter.
- Prevent repeated or invalid submissions.
- Update game progress and player scores.
- Award experience points and in-game rewards.
- Determine the outcomes of competitive duels.
- Detect suspicious automated gameplay.
- Maintain fair gameplay through anti-macro protection.

The bot may also process text-based commands when necessary to execute user-requested features.

CixGacha does not intentionally maintain a permanent archive of complete Discord conversations in its local gameplay databases.

However, previously submitted character names and relevant gameplay state information may be stored to maintain ongoing games and prevent repeated submissions.

Message content is not used for advertising, commercial profiling, or artificial intelligence model training.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by CixGacha is used to operate, maintain, and support the bot's functionality.

These purposes include:

- Providing interactive anime character games.
- Managing competitive player-versus-player duels.
- Tracking player progression and experience points.
- Maintaining collectible card inventories.
- Managing virtual currency and in-game transactions.
- Calculating global and server-specific leaderboards.
- Distributing daily rewards and collectible items.
- Detecting automated abuse and unfair gameplay.
- Verifying eligibility for server-specific rewards.
- Processing user-requested data deletion.
- Maintaining the stability and functionality of the gaming system.

CixGacha does not use Discord message content to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

We do not sell user information or use collected gameplay data for targeted advertising.

## 5. Server Membership Information

CixGacha uses Discord server membership information to provide accurate server-specific rankings and certain reward features.

For example, the bot may check whether a player belongs to a particular Discord server before including that player in a server-specific ranking.

The bot also provides level-based rewards that can be assigned to a Discord server selected by the player.

Membership checks help ensure that users can only select eligible servers in which they are members.

This information is processed to support the bot's features and is not used for advertising or unrelated user profiling.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

CixGacha stores gameplay information in local JSON data files managed by the bot operator.

Stored information may include:

- Discord user identifiers.
- Server identifiers.
- Experience points and levels.
- Player scores and virtual currency.
- Anime card inventories.
- Daily reward and participation information.
- Relevant game progression data.

Persistent gameplay records may remain stored for as long as necessary to maintain the bot's functionality, unless deleted following a valid user request or another applicable requirement.

The bot does not automatically delete all persistent gameplay records after a fixed period.

Temporary anti-abuse and security information is maintained in memory rather than in the persistent gameplay database.

This temporary information is generally scheduled for removal within 1 to 72 hours after relevant user activity, with periodic cleanup.

Certain shared game-state information, such as previously used character names, may remain stored to support ongoing gameplay.

## 7. Data Sharing

CixGacha does not sell users' personal information to third parties.

Certain gameplay information may be displayed publicly within Discord servers as part of the bot's functionality.

This may include:

- Player display names.
- Experience levels and rankings.
- Game scores and achievements.
- Competitive duel results.
- Collectible card information.

The bot operates through Discord's platform and may retrieve relevant Discord-hosted information, such as user avatars, to generate visual ranking cards and other features.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure necessary to operate the bot.

We do not intentionally share collected gameplay information with advertising companies.

## 8. User Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their stored personal gameplay records at any time.

CixGacha provides a dedicated Discord command for this purpose:

**/verilerimisil**

After a user confirms the deletion request, the bot attempts to remove the following associated information:

- Global XP and level data.
- Server-specific player points.
- Collected anime card inventories.
- Daily reward and participation records.
- Applicable temporary gameplay and security data.
- Associated active or pending duel information maintained by the bot.

This feature deletes personal gameplay records maintained by CixGacha.

It does not delete messages already hosted by Discord or unrelated shared game-state information.

If a user continues using the bot after deleting their data, new gameplay records may be created.

### Alternative Data Deletion Method

If CixGacha is offline, unavailable, or a user cannot access the data deletion command, they can request manual assistance through our official Discord support server.

**Official Support Server:** https://discord.gg/smmpvp

To submit a data deletion request:

1. Join our official Discord support server.
2. Open a private support ticket.
3. Explain that you wish to delete your CixGacha gameplay data.
4. Our development team will review the request, verify account ownership where necessary, and provide assistance with the deletion process.

Users may also open a support ticket to ask questions about the personal information processed by CixGacha.

Please do not share passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive account credentials when submitting requests.

## 9. Data Security

Access to stored gameplay information is managed by the bot operator.

We aim to protect user information against unauthorized access, misuse, and accidental disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If users have concerns about the security of their information, they may contact our development team through the official support server.

If we become aware of an inappropriate collection of personal information relating to an ineligible user, we will review the situation and take appropriate action.
